1. UGC character at brunch

PROMPT: Raw vertical 9:16 video recorded on a phone's front camera (not on screen), one continuous 30-second take with no cuts. It looks like an ordinary unedited phone clip, not a film: slightly soft image, a little digital noise and compression, deep focus with the café behind her as sharp as her face, auto-exposure brightening and dimming as people pass the sunny window behind her, no colour grading. The phone sits close and a little low, looking slightly up at her; around 9 s a waiter's arm bumps the table, the phone slides and tilts, and she catches it and props it back up. Sadie, a young woman in her early 20s with long honey-blonde waves, light natural makeup, small gold hoop earrings and a cropped cream knit top, sits at a small marble table by a big sunny window in a busy brunch café. Her sister sits across from her, out of frame except for her arm and a hand with a silver ring. Behind Sadie: other tables of people talking, a counter with a glass pastry case, plants, staff moving about, chalkboards too far away to read. Around 9 s a waiter sets down two plates, a lemon ricotta pancakes and a plate of eggs on toast, plus two iced coffees in plain glasses. Around 15 s she turns the pancake plate toward the camera; around 20 s she cuts a bite, eats it and closes her eyes; around 26 s her sister's hand reaches in with a fork and steals a piece of pancake, and Sadie swats at it, laughing. [Sadie: casual young woman's voice, warm and a bit giggly, recorded on the phone's built-in mic from half a metre away]: "Okay, I promised I'd show you what we get. ... We get the same thing every single time. ... Oh, here it is! Thank you! ... Look at this. Lemon ricotta pancakes. ... Mmm. Okay. Yeah. Still the best. ... Hey! Get your own!" [Her sister, off camera: a young woman's voice, dry and amused]: "That's the tax." [Sadie, laughing]: "She does this every time." Audio as captured by the phone mic in a busy café, not studio clean: her voice close but with the room around it, the hiss and grind of an espresso machine, cups and cutlery clinking, overlapping chatter and laughter from other tables, a door bell jingling, a chair scraping, faint music in the background. No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
